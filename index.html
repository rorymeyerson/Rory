Meet Avigail

About the Artist

Avigail Meyerson is an artist from New Jersey whose passion for creativity and artistic expression has been woven into her life from an early age. Working across pencil, oil, acrylic, and watercolor, she specializes in oil and watercolor painting, combining strong technical skill with a distinctive and expressive artistic voice.

Known especially for her ability to capture the likeness and character of her subjects, Avigail brings both precision and personality to her work. Her paintings are vibrant, intentional, and deeply personal—each one created with its own story and meaning.

This sense of depth is especially present in her Judaica, where Avigail draws inspiration from Jewish tradition, symbolism, and the stories behind the subjects she paints. Through rich color and thoughtful composition, she strives to create artwork that is not only beautiful, but meaningful—pieces that connect with the viewer on a deeper level.
